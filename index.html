Manhwa Chat Hub

v3.3.2 Latest Stable
Next-Gen Anime & Webtoon Comic Story Studio
Production Release • November 2026 • MIT Open Source

Manhwa Chat Hub is a dedicated creative studio built for animators, content creators, and webtoon authors to write, direct, and record viral chat stories disguised as authentic WhatsApp, ChatGPT 4o, and Google Gemini interfaces. Complete with cinematic auto-playback, Solo Leveling system quest alerts, emotion wardrobes, meme soundboards, and zero-loss offline storage.

⚡ Essential Screenplay Tags & Syntax

You can type or paste these tags directly into your chat or the AI Script Importer to trigger dynamic anime events:

[QUEST: ⚡ URGENT SURVIVAL MISSION: Evade Gian for 5 min!]
Spawns a glowing Solo Leveling holographic system quest window with dramatic sound effect!
[Nobita:shock]: What?! A System Quest on my phone?!
Automatically swaps Nobita's avatar to his shock face sprite for this line.
[Doraemon:laugh]: [STICKER: Dorayaki Feast]
Sends the paired reaction sticker with synchronized anime pop/fanfare audio!
[SCENE: chatgpt] or [SCENE: whatsapp]
Seamlessly auto-switches interfaces mid-playback when cinema reaches this line!
[BANNER: CHAPTER 2: ENTER THE DUNGEON]
Inserts a centered anime chapter title divider across the chat stream.
/delay 2.5sec
Forces a dramatic suspense pause before the next dialogue appears.
⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts (Directing Cheat Sheet)
Key Directing Action Purpose
H Toggle Pure Recording Mode Hides all studio headers, buttons & director dock for clean video recording.
Space Pause / Resume Playback Halts or continues the cinema playback timeline instantly.
Enter Send Message Adds the typed dialogue line directly into the chat stream.
Shift + Enter Multi-line Message Creates a new line within the same message bubble.
Esc Emergency Stop / Close Halts active cinema playback, stops audio, and closes any open modal window.
🎬 Directing Secrets for Viral Shorts & Reels
📱 9:16 Shorts Framing
Click the WhatsApp header to open Group Settings and select 9:16 Shorts/TikTok. This formats bubbles perfectly for mobile video feeds without letterboxing.
⚡ Speed Presets
Tap the ⚡ Speed Hub in the Cinema pill bar to switch between Slow (0.6x), Normal (1.0x), Fast (1.6x), or Instant playback for fast-paced comedic skits.
🎥 Auto-Camera Punch-In
Keep 🎥 Cam: ON to let the studio automatically punch-in zoom during dramatic lines, quest alerts, and climactic moments.
💾 One-Click JSON Backup
Always export a full studio backup before clearing messages. You can restore your entire cast, wardrobes, custom stickers, and stories at any time!
